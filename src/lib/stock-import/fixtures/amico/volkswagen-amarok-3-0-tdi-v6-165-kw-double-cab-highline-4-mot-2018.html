Vehicle overview

Discover the robust and stylish Volkswagen Amarok 3.0 TDI Highline 4Motion Auto Double-Cab, a perfect blend of power and comfort. Finished in a striking gold hue, this pick-up exudes confidence on every journey. With a smooth automatic transmission and a punchy 165kW diesel engine, it offers an exhilarating drive while maintaining impressive fuel efficiency at around 9.0 litres per 100 km. The Amarok's excellent condition with 181,000 km on the clock highlights its reliability and meticulous maintenance. Inside, you'll find luxurious leather seats, climate control, and satellite navigation for added convenience. Safety is paramount with four airbags, ABS, and an alarm system, ensuring peace of mind. Practical features like electric windows, cruise control, and a towbar make this vehicle versatile for work and leisure. Whether you're navigating city streets or heading off-road, this Amarok's 4Motion all-wheel drive system and a top speed of 193 km/h deliver exceptional performance. Brought to you by SA Multi Franchise Motor Group in Gauteng, this high-spec double-cab pickup is ready for new adventures.
